# LLMSecEval gold annotations (GPL-3.0)

Gold annotations of the LLMSecEval prompts (pilot and final sample). Each row holds the text
of a prompt of LLMSecEval (revision `39e97f046dbb2c4c604d31b86f2a4d3d27827388`), distributed
under GPL-3.0: this folder is therefore licensed under GPL-3.0 (see `LICENSE`, copied from the
dataset), not under the licence of the rest of the repository.

- `lse-pilot/`: the 12 pilot prompts; `lse-final/`: the 100 prompts of the sample.
- `annotations.jsonl`: rows in `pst-gold-annotations-v1`; `review-log.csv`: proposal of the
  LLM and final decision for every judgement unit; `provenance.json`: procedure and hashes.
